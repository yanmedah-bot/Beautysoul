About Beautysoul

Your time.
Your beauty.
Your soul.

Beautysoul is a small studio where beauty, wellness and self-care sit side by side. Come in for a haircut, a facial, your nails or a long massage — and leave with a little more time in your day that belonged only to you.

Every appointment starts with a conversation, so the treatment is shaped around what your hair and skin actually need. Quiet rooms, unhurried hands, and a space where you are welcome exactly as you are.

  • Personal attention at every appointment
  • A calm, relaxing experience from the moment you arrive
  • Professional service and quality treatments
  • A warm, welcoming environment for everyone
